How selection works

How We Select The Cohort.

The pilot Buildathon is limited to 100 builders. Applications are reviewed to create balanced teams with diverse skills and backgrounds.

The goal is not to select the most experienced people. The goal is to create the strongest possible collaborative environment.

Motivation

Ready to show up and build.

We look for applicants who want to create, not just attend.

Skill diversity

Range across the cohort.

Developers, designers, product thinkers, and AI builders in balance.

Team balance

Individual applications are placed to form capable, complementary teams.

Execution mindset

Focus on shipping.

The day rewards collaboration, clarity, and willingness to demo real work.

FAQ

Questions

Before You Apply.

Team

Do I need a team?

No. Apply as an individual and we'll help place you in a balanced team — or bring your own existing team.

Experience

Do I need experience?

No previous hackathon or advanced technical background required. Curiosity and commitment matter most.

Bring

What should I bring?

Laptop, charger, student ID, and the energy to build for a full day.

Food

Will food be provided?

Catering and logistics details are shared with confirmed builders before the event.

Cost

Is participation free?

Yes. The inaugural Buildathon is free for selected builders.

After

What happens after the event?

Selected teams may enter Guild Labs, mentorship, showcases, incubation, industry introductions, and WIL pathways.

Selection

How are teams selected?

We review applications to build balanced teams across skills, roles, and motivation — not just experience level.

Graduates

Can recent graduates apply?

Yes. Recent graduates connected to student communities are welcome to apply.
