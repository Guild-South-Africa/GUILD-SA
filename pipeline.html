02 / How it works

Discover. Learn. Register. Build. Demo. Continue. Exposure.

The pipeline turns first interest into shipped proof. Each step has a purpose, a constraint, and a visible output.

01 Discover

Find the node

Students learn what GUILD SA is, where the nearest campus guild lives, and why output matters.

02 Learn

Read the rules

No MVP = No Judgement. The format, criteria, and expectations are clear before teams commit.

03 Register

Form the team

Teams submit roles, institution, portfolio links, and an optional early idea signal.

04 Build

Heads down

Build blocks create focus. Mentors circulate, checkpoints redirect, and scope stays honest.

05 Demo

Prove it live

Teams pitch for 3-5 minutes, answer judge questions, and show working evidence.

06 Continue

Enter labs

Top teams receive structured support, mentorship, and a continuation path.

07 Exposure

Meet industry

Projects are published, shared with partners, and used as talent proof.

03

What gets produced

The pipeline ends in evidence, not applause.

Every serious team leaves with a clearer portfolio artifact: problem statement, MVP, demo link, stack, team roles, screenshots, and next-step recommendation.

Project card

Problem, solution, demo

A public snapshot that makes the work easy to inspect and share.

Team signal

Roles and decisions

Who designed, built, pitched, tested, and led under pressure.

Continuation

Labs or archive

Strong teams move forward. Other teams still leave with learning and proof.

Throughline

Each step reduces ambiguity.

Discovery clarifies fit. Registration clarifies commitment. Build blocks clarify capability. Demos clarify what is real. Continuation clarifies who is ready for more.
