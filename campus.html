Pilot node

Local campuses. Shared standards.

A Campus Guild is not a student club or event calendar. It is a structured operating layer embedded inside an institution, designed to support team formation, collaborative building, mentorship access, sprint environments, project capture, public demos, and continuation pathways.

Each node operates locally while remaining connected to a larger national ecosystem.
